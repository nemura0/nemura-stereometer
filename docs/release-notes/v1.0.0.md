The first release: a stereometer for Apple Silicon Macs, as an app and as a CLAP, VST3 and Audio
Unit plugin.

**App**
- A floating meter, fully transparent by default, that shows everything your Mac plays (Core
  Audio process tap, no driver) or one plugin instance sending from your DAW.
- Always on top, on every Space and over full-screen apps; click-through (⌃⌥⌘S) and show/hide
  (⌃⌥⌘H) from anywhere.
- Menu bar popover with the source picker, modes, presets, opacity and click-through; a Settings
  window with a live preview on the meter.

**Plugin**
- Zero latency, bit-exact pass-through; the meter in the editor, Send to app, or both.
- Settings saved with the project; Universal (arm64 and x86_64).

**Meter**
- Six modes: Goniometer, Goniometer (Log), Half goniometer, Half goniometer (Log), Lissajous and
  NEMURA.
- Correlation with a history strip and L/R balance; color by frequency, solid, amplitude or
  mid/side, six colormaps, and import of `.map` files.
- Factory presets Mix, Stage, NEMURA and Classic, shared by the app and the plugin.

**Install:** the `.dmg` holds the app; the `.pkg` installs the plugins (choose CLAP, VST3 and/or
AU). Homebrew: `brew install --cask nemura0/tap/stereometer` and `nemura0/tap/stereometer-plugins`.
Requires macOS 14.2 or later.
